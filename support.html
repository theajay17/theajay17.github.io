Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile › Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, likes and matches, chats, messages and chat photos (for both people in each chat), call history, block list, notifications and the purchase records linked to your account in our database.

If you cannot open the app, email us from the email address of your account and we will delete your account and data.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first — see Terms of Service.

Some information is kept after deletion: reports about safety violations, the free-trial records tied to your phone, and purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat. See the Privacy Policy.

Manage or cancel Bongo Pro

In the app: Profile › FAQ & Help › “How do I manage or cancel my subscription?” › Manage Subscription. Or in the Google Play Store: Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Bongo › Cancel subscription.

Report a safety concern

Use the report option on the profile or chat in the app, or email us. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. See our Safety & Child Protection policy.
