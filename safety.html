Safety & Child Protection

Last updated: November 2026

Bongo is committed to providing a safe, respectful community for adults. This policy brings together our safety rules, community guidelines, and our zero-tolerance approach to child protection. Everyone on Bongo is expected to follow it.

Adults only — zero tolerance for minors

Bongo is exclusively for people aged 18 and over. We have zero tolerance for any content or behaviour that sexualises, endangers or exploits minors.

  • Accounts belonging to anyone under 18 are removed immediately.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited and will be reported to the appropriate authorities.
  • Any attempt to groom, solicit or contact a minor is banned and reported.

Community guidelines

To keep Bongo positive for everyone, always:

  • Treat other members with respect and kindness.
  • Represent yourself honestly.
  • Ask for and respect consent in all interactions.
  • Keep conversations free of hate speech, harassment and threats.

Safety rules

Protect yourself while meeting new people:

  • Never send money or share financial information with other users.
  • Be careful about sharing your home address or workplace early on.
  • Meet in public places and tell a friend where you are going.
  • Trust your instincts and leave any situation that feels unsafe.

Prohibited behaviour

The following are not allowed on Bongo:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech.
  • Nudity or sexually explicit content in public profiles.
  • Scams, solicitation or requests for money.
  • Impersonation, fake profiles or spam.
  • Any illegal activity or promotion of self-harm or violence.

Reporting & blocking

You are our most important safety partner. If someone breaks these rules:

  • Use the report option so our safety team can review the account.
  • Block any user you do not want to interact with.
  • In an emergency, contact your local authorities immediately.

Reports are confidential, and our team reviews them to keep the community safe.

Enforcement

We investigate reports and may warn, suspend or permanently ban accounts that break these rules. Serious violations, especially those involving minors, are reported to law enforcement.
