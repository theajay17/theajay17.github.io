Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bongo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bongo app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It also explains how payments for Bongo Pro and advertising are handled. By using Bongo you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Bongo is a gay dating app available on Google Play. In this policy, “Bongo”, “we” and “us” mean the Bongo app and its developer. For any privacy question or request, email amit212526@gmail.com or use Profile › Contact Us in the app.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in: if you sign in with Google, your name, email address and Google account ID; if you create an account with an email address and password, your email address. This is used only to create and secure your Bongo account, and is held by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile: display name, age, bio, the profile picture you choose from Bongo's built-in pictures (one is assigned automatically until you pick your own), position, height, weight, meeting preferences and app language. Your exact date of birth stays on your device; only your age is stored on our servers.
  • Location: with your permission, your phone's location is used on your device to show distances. Our servers store only a rounded location (accurate to about 1 km) and the name of your area or city. You can also type your area yourself in Profile › Edit Profile.
  • Activity: likes, matches, chats, photos you send in chat, call history, blocks and the reports you submit.
  • Free-message counter: how many free messages you have sent today and how many rewarded ads you have watched today, so the daily allowance can be applied.
  • Device data: notification token, online status and last-seen time.
  • Advertising identifiers: see “Advertising” below.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.
  • Subscription data: see “Payments & subscription data” below.

Sensitive information

Because Bongo is a gay dating app, having a Bongo account — and details such as your position and meeting preferences — can reveal your sexual orientation and sexual preferences. This is sensitive information. You decide what to add to your profile, and by creating a profile you agree that we use it to run Bongo as described here. We never sell it, and we never share it with advertisers or use it to target ads. You can remove it at any time by deleting your account.

Advertising

Bongo shows optional rewarded video ads: you can choose to watch a short ad to get a few more free messages for the day. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads are requested as non-personalised. That means AdMob does not use a profile of your interests to choose which ad to show you.
  • To serve and measure an ad, and to prevent ad fraud, Google receives technical data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, app version and your interaction with the ad.
  • We never send your profile, your sexual orientation, your messages, your photos or your location to any advertiser or ad network.
  • Ad content is limited to a PG rating.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Subscribers to Bongo Pro are not shown rewarded ads, because they are not limited by the daily free-message allowance.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Payments & subscription data

All payments for Bongo Pro (including UPI apps such as PhonePe, Google Pay and Paytm, and cards) are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to confirm and manage your Bongo Pro plan.

  • We never receive or store your card number, UPI ID, UPI PIN or bank account details.
  • From Google Play, through RevenueCat, we receive only the purchase status: a purchase token, the plan, whether you are in the free trial, the renewal or expiry date and whether auto-renew is on or cancelled.
  • Your purchases are linked to your Bongo account ID, which we share with RevenueCat so that a subscription works only on the account that bought it.
  • We use this data only to unlock Bongo Pro, show your plan status, prevent fraud and offer the free trial only once per account. Google Play applies its own limit of one free trial per Google account.

Google's handling of your payment data is covered by Google's own Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. RevenueCat handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and profile.
  • Show nearby profiles, and enable likes, chats and calls.
  • Apply the daily free-message allowance and credit rewarded ads.
  • Provide and verify your Bongo Pro subscription and prevent free-trial abuse.
  • Send notifications about messages, likes, matches, calls, your account and safety, plus — if you turn them on — occasional reminders and news about Bongo.
  • Keep the community safe, review reports and enforce our Terms.
  • Answer your support requests.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in Bongo users can see your profile: name, age, profile picture, bio, position, meeting preferences, area or city name, approximate distance and whether you are online.
  • The profile record that Bongo loads on other members' phones also contains your height, weight, app language, rounded location, last-seen time and your Bongo Pro plan status.
  • Your email address and phone number are not part of your profile record and are not visible to other users.
  • When you like someone, they can see that you liked them.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

  • Service providers that run Bongo for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, push notifications and app settings), Supabase (private photo storage, in-app notifications and server functions), ZEGOCLOUD (voice and video calls), RevenueCat (subscription management), Google Play (payments) and Google AdMob (rewarded ads, as described above).
  • If you use Bongo in a language other than English, Bongo's own app text is translated with Google's translation service. Your messages, your profile and other members' names are never sent for translation.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside your country.

Chats, photos & calls

  • Chat photos are kept in private storage that only the two people in the chat can open. View-once photos can be opened only once and are then deleted from our servers.
  • Disappearing messages are hidden from the chat 24 hours after they are sent, and are deleted from our servers. Only messages sent while the setting is on are affected.
  • “Delete chat” hides a conversation only for you; the other person keeps their copy.
  • Voice and video calls are live connections; Bongo does not record them.
  • On Android, Bongo blocks screenshots and screen recording inside the app. People can still capture what you share, for example by photographing the screen with another phone, so share with care.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Profile › Delete Account. This permanently deletes:

  • your Bongo account and profile;
  • your likes and matches, including your likes on other people's profiles;
  • your chats, messages and chat photos — for both people in each chat;
  • your call history, block list, notifications and the purchase records linked to your account in our database.

Some information is kept after your account is deleted:

  • Reports about safety violations, to protect other users and meet legal obligations.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, for accounting and fraud prevention.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (see our Terms of Service).

If you can't use the app, email amit212526@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we will delete your account and data.

Your rights & choices

  • Edit your profile at any time in Profile › Edit Profile.
  • Turn location and notifications on or off in your phone's settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Block and report any user.
  • Delete your account in Profile › Delete Account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, by emailing amit212526@gmail.com or through Profile › Contact Us.

Children's privacy

Bongo is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we remove it. See our Safety & Child Protection policy.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, chat photos open only through short-lived private links, and access to your data is restricted by strict server rules. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Important changes will be shown in the app. Continued use of Bongo after an update means you accept the revised policy.
